Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

The Daily Seven is a free daily quiz game at thedailyseven.com, run by an individual based in Ireland. This page explains, in plain words, what information the game uses and why. In short: no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking. The only thing that leaves your device is your daily score for the leaderboard.

Stored on your own device

The game saves a few things in your browser's local storage so it can remember your game. They stay on your device and are not sent to us:

  • Today's progress and round scores, so you can come back to an unfinished game.
  • Your streak, games played, best score and practice stats.
  • A random device code (for example 3f2a9c1e-…) and your leaderboard name, used for the leaderboard below.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

The worldwide leaderboard

When you finish the daily game, it sends your result to our leaderboard database so it can show your rank and the top 10. We store:

WhatWhy
Your leaderboard name (a random “Player 1234” unless you change it)Shown publicly in the top 10
Your total and round scores, and the puzzle dayTo rank players and work out “you did better than x%”
The random device codeSo one device counts once per day and can change its name
The time you submittedTo break ties

We don't ask for your email, real name or location, and we can't link the device code to you. Your leaderboard name is public, so please don't use your full real name.

We do this on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a leaderboard that players expect from a daily game. Scores are kept while the leaderboard runs. To have your entries removed, email us with your leaderboard name and the dates you played.

Services that help run the site

Like any website, the services below receive your device's IP address when your browser connects to them. We use them only to deliver the game:

  • Render: hosts the website files.
  • Cloudflare: manages our domain name (DNS).
  • Supabase: stores the leaderboard database.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts.
  • jsDelivr: provides the map code used in the map rounds.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU under their own safeguards. Each has its own privacy policy.

Cookies and advertising

The Daily Seven currently uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. If that changes, we'll update this page first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete data about you, or object to how we use it. Email us at hello@thedailyseven.com (hello@thedailyseven.com). You can also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal details. We ask younger players not to use their real name on the leaderboard.

Changes

If we change how the game uses data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
